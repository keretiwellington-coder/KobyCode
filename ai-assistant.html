An assistant on your website that answers customers for you.

Visitors ask questions at all hours. Your assistant answers using the information you give it, and passes serious requests to you.

The problem

Most visitors leave when their question goes unanswered

People check a website at night, on weekends and between jobs. If they cannot find the price, the opening hours or the next step, many simply move on to the next business.

Questions arrive when you are busy

You cannot reply to every visitor the moment they ask. An assistant can.

The same questions come up again and again

Prices, hours, location and what you offer. The assistant handles the repeats so you do not have to.

Interested visitors should reach you

When a visitor is serious, the assistant passes the request on so you can follow up.

How it works

It only answers from what you have told it

You give the assistant information about your business. It uses that information to answer visitors, in a chat window on your website.

You share your information

Your services, prices, opening hours, location and the questions customers ask most.

The assistant learns it

We load that information into the assistant so its answers match your business.

Visitors ask, it answers

Visitors type a question in the chat window and get an answer straight away, at any hour.

Serious requests reach you

When a visitor wants something the assistant cannot settle, it passes the request on to you.

Good to know

Honest answers about what it can and cannot do

Does the assistant know everything about my business?
No. It only knows what you give it. If a visitor asks something outside that information, it should say so and pass the request to you, which is why keeping the information current matters.
Does it replace me or my staff?
No. It handles the repeat questions and passes the real conversations to you. You still make the decisions and close the work.
Can I change what it knows?
Yes. When your prices, hours or services change, tell us and the monthly maintenance covers updating its information.
What does the monthly maintenance cover?
Keeping the assistant running and keeping its information up to date. The exact details are written in your proposal.
Do I need a KobyCode website to use it?
The assistant is added to a KobyCode website. If you do not have one yet, we can build the website and the assistant together.
Can I see it before I decide?
Yes. Open the live demo above, and message us if you have questions about how it would work for your business.
