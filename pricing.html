Pricing

Clear prices in your own currency

Each currency has its own set price. These are business prices, not automatic conversions.

Prices shown are starting prices. Your final quote depends on the agreed scope. Hosting, domain names and third-party tools are not included unless your proposal says so.

Maintenance

Website Care Plans

Optional monthly plans to keep your website up to date after launch. Support allowances are limited and clearly defined, never unlimited.

Care Plans cover routine checks and small changes such as text, photos, prices and opening hours. New features, substantial redesigns, hosting, premium software licences and other third-party costs are excluded unless your agreement explicitly includes them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?
Websites start at for a Starter site, for a Business site and for a Premium site. Larger projects such as custom applications are quoted by scope. Prices are starting prices, and your final quote depends on the agreed scope.
How long does development take?
Starter sites usually take 5–10 working days, Business sites 10–15 working days and Premium sites 3–5 weeks. Custom projects are timed after scoping. The clock starts once the scope, price and payment terms are agreed and your content has been received.
Can I pay in Nigerian naira?
Yes. Every package has its own naira price. Choose NGN in the currency selector to see it.
Can international clients pay in USD, EUR, or GBP?
Yes. Each package has set prices in US dollars, euros and pounds. The payment method is confirmed in your proposal before any work begins.
Do you provide hosting?
We help you choose and set up hosting and a domain name. Those costs are paid to the provider and are not included in the package price unless your proposal says so. Many simple websites can run on free or low-cost hosting.
Can I update my website myself?
By default, sites are built as fast static pages and changes are made by KobyCode, either through a Care Plan or as a one-off request. If you need to edit content yourself, tell us in your inquiry and we can scope it as a custom project.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes, through optional monthly Care Plans from per month. Each plan has a defined allowance of changes. It is optional, and you can also request one-off changes.
What happens if I need additional features?
Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before any work starts, so you always know the cost first.
Who owns the website after completion?
Once the final payment is made, you own your website's content and the finished design and code delivered to you. Third-party tools, fonts and licences keep their own terms.
What happens after I submit an inquiry?
Your inquiry is saved and reviewed personally. You'll get a reply by email, or by WhatsApp if you ask, with questions or a proposal. No work starts until the scope, price and payment terms are agreed.
